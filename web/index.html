MACHINE SETUP

Motion calibration

UNO · 115200

Set the driver microstepping and belt geometry, then place the carriage at the physical origin and zero the counters. X uses STEP/DIR/EN on D2/D3/D10; Y uses A0/A1/A2. Jog with every pen raised. Drivers release after each action and after five seconds idle.

Motor and driver settings match the hardware
Pulse choices match your photographed DM556 table with 200 full steps/rev. Turn driver power off before changing SW5–8, then select the matching setting here and zero the carriage again. Selection rescales steps/mm; the app cannot change these switches.Keep SW1–SW3 current settings unchanged. The photographed table starts at 1.4 A peak; verify compatibility with the motor’s reported 0.62 A rating before a loaded speed test. SW4 OFF selects half standstill current and does not reduce moving current.
Eased acceleration plotting & jogs
Smooth acceleration and braking. Longer ramps are gentler; lower corner speed helps sharp turns. Compatible queued paths carry speed forward. Requires motion firmware v5.

Servo angles

Test one color at a time. Servo signals are red D6, blue D7, green D8, and black D11. Use an external 5 V servo supply and share ground with the Uno.
